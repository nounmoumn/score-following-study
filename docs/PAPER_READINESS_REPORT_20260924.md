## Final scientific verdict

1. **P1 remains a weak descriptive result.** Static score ambiguity correlates modestly with error but does not reliably select interventions.
2. **P2 remains a context result, not an action rule.** Two seconds of future context improves past localization on average, but ambiguity does not identify where it helps.
3. **semi-P3 remains conditional mechanism evidence.** It constrains interpretation but does not yield a general ground-truth-free alert.
4. **Dynamic relocation validation succeeds only in the symbolic pipeline.** It passes a frozen work/performer-disjoint ASAP lockbox.
5. **Audio rescue fails.** The trigger is sparse and tracker/repertoire-specific, the frozen model never holds, and the prospective mean-evidence rule makes regret worse.
6. **The algorithmic principle is not novel.** The publishable angle is decision utility plus a frozen cross-domain failure and trigger-coverage audit.

## Submission judgment

The package is suitable for an honest Late-Breaking/Demo or workshop-style empirical paper after formatting and author metadata are added. It is not yet a strong full-paper algorithm contribution. A stronger future full paper would require a genuinely new audio corpus or tracker family, a candidate-evidence model fixed before outcomes, and validation on new works—not additional tuning on Vienna 4x22.

## Closed search space

Do not tune jump threshold, delay, chroma margin, DTW constraint, or feature selection on the opened ASAP or Vienna outcomes. Do not relabel the failed gates. Any further audio method must begin with a new protocol and new works.
